# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, discussions and pull requests.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith and help newcomers learn.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberately disruptive or bad-faith participation

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **qq591277494@126.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, close threads or block participants who violate this code.
